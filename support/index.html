Common questions

The shot fires before I let go. Open Settings and raise PINCH RELEASE.

My hands are not tracked well. Play in a well-lit room. Cueball shows a warning when the light is too low for hand tracking.

How do I replay the tutorial? Press TUTORIAL on the main menu.
