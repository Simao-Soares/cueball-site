Privacy Policy

Effective 18 November 2026

This policy covers Cueball, a game for Meta Quest published by Badjora Studios (“we”). Cueball is a single-player game. We do not run servers for it and we do not collect personal data.

What we collect

Nothing. Cueball has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics. We do not receive your name, email address, location, voice or play data.

Data that stays on your headset

  • Game progress and settings (saved runs, best runs, options) are stored only on your headset. Uninstalling the game deletes them.
  • Hand tracking is used to control the game. Hand data is provided by your headset in real time and is not stored or sent anywhere by Cueball.
  • Passthrough and room data (the layout of your walls, floor and furniture, if you allow access to spatial data) is used on the headset to place the game and let balls bounce off your room. Cueball does not store, upload or share it, and it never receives camera images.

Game engine

Cueball is built with the Unity engine. Unity may receive anonymous technical information about the device when the game starts, such as the headset model and system version, under the Unity privacy policy. This does not identify you.

Meta

Cueball is distributed through the Meta Horizon Store. Meta processes data such as your account and purchases under its own privacy policy. We do not control that data.

Children

Cueball does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
