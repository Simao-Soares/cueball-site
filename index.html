Cueball

Pool meets poker in a glass cube floating in your room.

Cueball is a mixed-reality roguelike for Meta Quest, played with your bare hands. Pinch the cue ball, pull back like a plunger and let go. It flies into the cube and scatters the rack in three dimensions. All eight corners are pockets, and you can turn the cube to line up the shot you want.

Every ball you sink lands on your shelf. Bank it as a poker hand: pairs, flushes, straights, up to Five of a Kind. Score is chips × mult. A shot that sinks nothing costs you your hand, and the 8 ball burns it.

Beat each round to reach the shop. Build your deck with mods, editions, single-use cheats and prizes from the claw machine, then survive the boss round.

Features

  • Hand tracking only, no controllers needed
  • Mixed reality: the cube floats in your own room
  • Seated play with short, replayable runs
  • Guided tutorial and a relaxed Zen mode
